UNKICKS WEBSITE — START HERE

Included:
- index.html: complete responsive storefront demo
- README.txt: setup and launch notes

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
3. An internet connection is needed for the product photos.

WHAT WORKS IN THIS DEMO
- Responsive homepage and mobile navigation
- Shop categories and product filtering
- Search products
- Add to bag, quantity controls, subtotal
- WhatsApp order message preparation (requires your business number)

BEFORE GOING LIVE
1. Replace the sample product names, prices, and photos with your real inventory.
2. In index.html, find WHATSAPP_NUMBER and add your business number with country code and digits only, e.g. 2348012345678.
3. Verify all prices, sizes, stock, delivery, return and contact details.
4. Connect a real payment provider/backend if you want online card/bank payments, accounts, inventory management, and saved orders.
5. Publish the website through a hosting provider. The local file is not publicly online by itself.

IMPORTANT
Product prices and photos are demonstration placeholders, not confirmed Unkicks inventory. Some sample photos may need replacing to ensure they match the exact footwear being sold. Newsletter signup is a visual demo and does not store email addresses. No payment details are collected by this demo.
